# shopping-behavior

## Analytical Questions

What are 6 analytical questions you can ask about this dataset?

1) Which customers are the most frequent shoppers?
    Is there a pattern in what type of items they buy? Do they buy the same thing each time? A basket of items? Or is there variability? Is there a pattern to how frequently they buy?


2) What age cohort spends the most money with our company?
    Does the top spending cohort have a preference for item type or color? What is the average price per item that they spend on?

3) What item type is the most popular? Is it most popular among any particular age or gender category? 

4) What locations are most of our sales coming from?

5) What is the busiest season for the company, and what sells most during that season? 

6) Does each person spend on average the same amount of money per shopping experience? Or is there a distinction between people who tend to spend a lot more or a lot less than others? What is a common variable amongst these customers?
